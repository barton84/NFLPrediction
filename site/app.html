Quarterbacks and injuries

QB values are tested: the model rates every QB by career EPA per dropback (recent seasons count more) and moves the spread by about points per 0.1 EPA difference from the QB the team has been playing with. Non-QB values are rough position priors, scaled down for doubtful and questionable. They are not backtested. Your entries are saved in this browser only.

Game-day weather

Weather moves the projected total only. Bad weather did not make NFL games any closer in the backtest, so it does not touch the spread. Not a live forecast: look up the stadium forecast and enter it here.

Model weights

Defaults are fitted on 2016-2025. The range under each slider is what the weight came out to when each season was held out in turn. Tight ranges mean the signal is stable. Changing a slider updates the board above too.
