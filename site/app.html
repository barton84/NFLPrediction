Closing line value

After the model posts its number, does the betting line move toward the model's side by kickoff? When it does, the market is agreeing with the model, which is usually the first sign an edge is real. The first number is how often that happens. The second is how often the model's side covered in those games.

Is the cover chance honest?

Every 2016-2025 game, grouped by the cover chance the page would have shown for the model's side, next to how often that side actually covered. Each season was scored by a version fitted on the other nine.
